# Scenario 1 — The Saturday Forest Clean-up

## Objective
To find and register for a local environmental event within a limited weekend timeframe.

## Actors
Eshwar, a 23-year-old MSc student living in Germany who wants to participate in sustainability activities while balancing university life.

## Activities

It is Friday afternoon, and Eshwar finishes his university lectures earlier than expected. While travelling home on the U-Bahn, he opens the EcoConnect website on his smartphone to search for nearby volunteering opportunities for the weekend.

He uses the event filters to search for activities happening "Tomorrow" and "Within 5 km." The platform displays several nearby events, including a "Forest Litter Clean-up" event scheduled for Saturday morning in a local park. The event page clearly shows the meeting location, duration, required equipment, weather expectations, and the number of volunteers already attending.

Eshwar clicks the "Quick Register" button, and the platform automatically fills in his saved profile information, including his university email address. After registering successfully, he uses the "Sync to Calendar" feature so that the event appears in his personal calendar application.

Later that evening, EcoConnect sends him a reminder notification containing the event time, location, and recommended items to bring such as gloves and sturdy shoes.

On Saturday morning, the system sends a final location pin and navigation link to help him reach the correct meeting point without confusion.

## Problem / Gap

Before EcoConnect, Eshwar usually discovered volunteering opportunities through outdated websites, university bulletin boards, or social media posts. These methods often lacked accurate timings, map locations, or reminder systems, causing confusion and missed events.

This scenario also revealed a new system gap: outdoor environmental events are heavily dependent on weather conditions and accurate location updates. Existing systems do not provide real-time emergency notifications when meeting points or schedules change unexpectedly.

## Resolution

EcoConnect solves this problem by combining event discovery, quick registration, reminders, calendar synchronisation, and location services within a single platform.

The platform also supports organiser announcements and emergency push notifications so that users receive immediate updates regarding weather-related cancellations, delays, or meeting-point changes.

# Reflection — Gap Identified

Writing this scenario revealed an important missing requirement that we as a team had not initially considered:

## Real-Time Navigation & Weather Alerts

Since environmental volunteering activities are often outdoors, EcoConnect may eventually require integration with:
- GPS and map services
- Weather APIs
- Push notification systems

These integrations would allow organisers to notify volunteers immediately if weather conditions become unsafe or if event locations change unexpectedly.

# Scenario 2 — Weekend Volunteering for a Working Professional

## Objective
To find short-duration volunteering opportunities that fit around a full-time work schedule.

## Actors
Preetham, a 30-year-old IT employee in Berlin who wants flexible environmental volunteering opportunities during weekends.

## Activities

After a busy work week, Preetham opens EcoConnect on Saturday morning to search for volunteering events that require only a few hours of commitment. He filters events by "Weekend," "Under 3 Hours," and "Near Berlin."

He finds a community recycling campaign taking place in a nearby neighbourhood. The event page clearly displays the start time, expected duration, volunteer limit, and organiser details.

Preetham checks the reviews and event description before registering. He appreciates that the platform clearly explains what tasks volunteers will perform and how much physical activity is involved.

After attending the event, he receives a contribution summary on his dashboard showing his completed volunteering activity.

## Problem / Gap

Many volunteering platforms provide unclear event schedules or require long-term commitments, making them unsuitable for busy professionals. Users like Preetham may avoid volunteering entirely if events appear disorganised or time-consuming.

## Resolution

EcoConnect addresses this issue by allowing detailed event filtering, displaying clear commitment levels, and showing transparent event information before registration.

# Scenario 3 — Community Networking Through Environmental Events

## Objective
To participate in environmental activities while meeting like-minded people in the local community.

## Actors
Simon, a 45-year-old guitarist and community artist interested in environmental and social engagement.

## Activities

Simon searches EcoConnect for community-oriented environmental events with relaxed and welcoming atmospheres. He discovers a riverside clean-up event followed by a small community gathering organised by local volunteers.

The event page includes details about the environmental activity as well as optional social interactions afterwards. Simon joins the event and uses the platform to save future activities that match his interests.

During the event, he meets local residents and other artists who share similar interests in sustainability and music.

## Problem / Gap

Most volunteering platforms focus only on task completion and fail to support the social and emotional aspects of community participation.

## Resolution

EcoConnect encourages community-building by allowing organisers to include social and mental well-being information within event descriptions, helping users feel more comfortable and connected.
